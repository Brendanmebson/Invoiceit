Invoiceit documentation

The rules Invoiceit follows: who can do what, how invoices are numbered, how every total is worked out, and what happens when a subscription ends.

On this page Accounts and roles Join codes Invoices How totals work Payments Regions and currencies Trial and billing Invoice data API quickstart

Accounts and roles

Someone creates an account with an email, a password and a business name. That person becomes the business's first owner. Everyone else joins an existing business with a join code from an owner.

Each account belongs to exactly one business. A business can have several owners and any number of staff.

RoleCan do
OwnerEverything staff can do, plus: open the Dashboard and Business pages, edit the business name, address and phone, make and cancel join codes, remove people from the team, and pay for the subscription.
StaffWrite invoices, view the business's invoices, record payments and share or print invoices. Staff never see or change business details or billing.

On an owner's first sign-in, Invoiceit asks for the business address and phone number once. They print at the top of every invoice, so staff never have to type them.

Join codes

Email confirmation is off, so an email address alone doesn't prove someone works for a business. Instead, an owner makes a join code on the Business page and gives it to the new person, who enters it when they sign up.

  • A code is 8 characters, letters and numbers. Case doesn't matter when it's typed in.
  • Each code works once. It's deleted as soon as someone joins with it.
  • Codes expire after 7 days if nobody uses them. Owners can also cancel a code before then.
  • An owner chooses the role when making the code: New staff code or New owner code.

Invoices

Numbering

Invoice numbers are five digits (00001, 00002, …) and count up separately for each business. The database gives an invoice its number at the moment it's saved, so two people saving on two devices at the same time never get the same number. The number shown while writing an invoice is a preview, and it can change if someone else saves first.

Once saved, an invoice's number never changes.

What an invoice holds

  • Customer name and mobile number, the date and time, and who made the sale.
  • Items: a description, an optional serial number, a quantity and a unit price for each line.
  • Discount, delivery and VAT, all optional.
  • Payments received so far, and notes.
  • A copy of the business name, address and phone as they were when the invoice was written. Changing business details later doesn't change invoices that already exist.

Changing an invoice

After an invoice is saved, you can add payments to it until it's fully paid. To bill a customer again for the same items, use Duplicate invoice: it copies the customer and items into a new invoice with a new number and no payments.

How totals work

Every figure on an invoice is worked out the same way, in this order:

Subtotal  = sum of (quantity × unit price) for each item
Discount  = the discount, but never more than the subtotal
VAT       = (Subtotal − Discount) × VAT % ÷ 100
Total     = Subtotal − Discount + Delivery + VAT
Paid      = sum of all payments
Balance   = Total − Paid   (never below 0)
  • A blank quantity counts as 1.
  • VAT is charged on the discounted subtotal. Delivery is added after and isn't taxed.
  • Total, paid and balance are rounded to 2 decimal places.

Status

StatusWhen
PAIDThe total is above 0 and the balance is 0.
PART-PAIDSome money has been paid, but there's a balance left.
UNPAIDNothing has been paid yet.

Payments

An invoice can have any number of payments, so a customer can pay in parts. Each payment has an amount, a date and a method: Cash, Bank Transfer or POS.

  • When writing a new invoice, the first payment follows the total until you type in it, which covers the usual case of a customer paying in full.
  • Payments can't add up to more than the total. A new invoice with overpayments won't save, and a later payment can't be more than the balance left.
  • Before a later payment is saved, Invoiceit re-reads the invoice, so a payment recorded on another device a moment earlier isn't lost or counted twice.

Recording a payment only updates the invoice. Invoiceit doesn't take or move your customers' money.

Regions and currencies

Each business has a region. It's picked once, at sign-up, from the device's time zone (Invoiceit doesn't look up IP addresses), and after that only the server can change it. The region sets two things: the default currency for new invoices, and the subscription price.

RegionDefault invoice currencySubscription
NigeriaNaira (₦)₦20,000 a month
United StatesUS dollar ($)$15 a month
United KingdomPound (£)$15 a month
Euro countriesEuro (€)$15 a month
Everywhere elseUS dollar ($)$15 a month

The default is only a starting point. Any invoice can be switched to ₦, $, £ or € while it's being written. The Dashboard shows totals in the business's default currency.

Trial and billing

  1. Days 0 to 14: free trial. Full access, no card needed. An email address that has already had a trial doesn't get a second one.
  2. Paying. An owner can pay at any time, for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months. Each month is 30 days, added after the trial ends or on top of time already paid for, so paying early never loses days.
  3. Access ends. When the trial or paid time runs out, the business is locked: the database stops returning its invoices, team and codes. Owners see a pay button when they sign in; staff are asked to contact their owner.
  4. 14 days after access ends without payment (day 28 for a business that never paid), the business is deleted along with all of its invoices, team and join codes. This can't be undone.

There's no scheduled job for deletion. The clean-up runs whenever anyone signs in to Invoiceit, so a business is deleted the next time the app is used after its deadline.

Invoice data

This is how a saved invoice looks inside Invoiceit. It's published to explain the model. It isn't a public API contract and may change.

{

  "no": "00042",                    // set by the database, never changes
  "date": "08-11-2026 02:15 PM",
  "cust": "Ada Okafor",
  "phone": "0803 000 0000",
  "salesBy": "Tunde",
  "currency": "NGN",
  "store": { "name": "…", "addr": "…", "phone": "…" },
  "items": [
    { "desc": "Phone case", "serial": "", "qty": "2", "price": "3500" }
  ],
  "discount": "500",
  "delivery": "1500",
  "tax": "7.5",                     // VAT, in percent
  "payments": [
    { "method": "Cash", "amount": "5000", "date": "08-11-2026" }
  ],

Amounts are stored as the text typed in and are turned into numbers when totals are worked out. Totals, balance and status aren't stored; they're always worked out again from the items and payments.

API quickstart

The API creates and reads invoices for your business from your own systems. Base URL:

https://hpytwigybqjmolacnerg.supabase.co/functions/v1/invoiceit-api

Every endpoint, field and error is in the API reference (also available as openapi.json).

1. Get a key

In Invoiceit, the owner opens Business → API, names a key and taps Create key. Copy it straight away: it's shown once. Keep it on your server, never in a web page or app.

2. Create an invoice

Uses 1 token. Your business details and the next invoice number are filled in for you. The Idempotency-Key makes retries safe.

curl -X POST https://hpytwigybqjmolacnerg.supabase.co/functions/v1/invoiceit-api/v1/invoices \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $INVOICEIT_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: order-10482" \
  -d '{"customer":{"name":"Amaka Eze"},"currency":"NGN",
       "items":[{"description":"Office desk","quantity":1,"unit_price":185000}],
       "payments":[{"method":"bank_transfer","amount":100000}]}'
// JavaScript (Node 18+, server side)
const res = await fetch("https://hpytwigybqjmolacnerg.supabase.co/functions/v1/invoiceit-api/v1/invoices", {
  method: "POST",
  headers: {
    Authorization: `Bearer ${process.env.INVOICEIT_KEY}`,
    "Content-Type": "application/json",
    "Idempotency-Key": "order-10482",
  },
  body: JSON.stringify({
    customer: { name: "Amaka Eze" },
    currency: "NGN",
    items: [{ description: "Office desk", quantity: 1, unit_price: 185000 }],
    payments: [{ method: "bank_transfer", amount: 100000 }],
  }),
});
const body = await res.json();
if (!res.ok) throw new Error(`${body.error.code}: ${body.error.message}`);
console.log(body.data.number, body.data.balance, res.headers.get("X-Tokens-Remaining"));
# Python (requests)
import os, requests
r = requests.post(
    "https://hpytwigybqjmolacnerg.supabase.co/functions/v1/invoiceit-api/v1/invoices",
    headers={"Authorization": f"Bearer {os.environ['INVOICEIT_KEY']}", "Idempotency-Key": "order-10482"},
    json={"customer": {"name": "Amaka Eze"}, "currency": "NGN",
          "items": [{"description": "Office desk", "quantity": 1, "unit_price": 185000}],
          "payments": [{"method": "bank_transfer", "amount": 100000}]},
)
data = r.json()
if not r.ok:
    raise RuntimeError(f"{data['error']['code']}: {data['error']['message']}")
print(data["data"]["number"], data["data"]["balance"])

The response has numbers and ISO dates, plus the worked-out subtotal, tax, total, paid, balance and status (PAID, UNPAID or PART-PAID). Fetch links.html_url with your key and save the response as a .html file to get a printable A4 page. (Supabase sends it as plain text, so a browser won't display the URL directly.)

3. Check your tokens

curl https://hpytwigybqjmolacnerg.supabase.co/functions/v1/invoiceit-api/v1/usage -H "Authorization: Bearer $INVOICEIT_KEY"

Every response also carries an X-Tokens-Remaining header.

4. Handle errors

Errors always look like {"error": {"code", "message", "details"}}.

StatusCodeWhat to do
401unauthorizedCheck the key. Revoked keys stop working at once.
402tokens_exhaustedUpgrade in Business → API, or wait for the reset date in details.
402subscription_inactiveRenew the Invoiceit subscription.
404not_foundNo such invoice in your business.
422validation_failed, overpaymentFix the fields listed in details. Nothing was saved and no token was used.
429rate_limitedWait for the seconds in Retry-After. Limit: 60 requests a minute per key.
500internal_errorRetry with the same Idempotency-Key.

PDF output isn't available yet: GET /v1/invoices/{id}/pdf returns 501. Use the /html page and print or save it as PDF.

What the API stores

  • Keys are hashed. Invoiceit keeps only a SHA-256 hash of each key, its name and its first 8 characters. The full key is shown once and can't be recovered; revoke it and make a new one.
  • Invoices created through the API are stored like any other invoice, for as long as the business exists. Each one records which key made it (api: and the key's first 8 characters).
  • Changes to an invoice keep a copy of the data from before the change, with who made it, so the owner can see what changed.
  • A call log of every request (time, endpoint, result, tokens used, invoice id) is kept for the owner's API page. Request bodies aren't logged.
  • Idempotency records, which hold the response of a create request, are deleted after 24 hours.
  • Deletion. If a business stays unpaid for 14 days after its access ends, everything above is permanently deleted with it.
