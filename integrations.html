What Invoiceit connects to

The services Invoiceit works with today, what each one does, and exactly what gets shared with it.

On this page Paystack WhatsApp Print and PDF Invoice links Supabase Not yet available

Paystack Live

Invoiceit's own subscription is paid through Paystack. This is how a business pays for Invoiceit; it isn't used to collect money from your customers.

How a payment goes

  1. An owner presses pay on the Business page or the "trial ended" screen and chooses 1, 3, 6 or 12 months. The trial banner's Pay now button pays for 1 month.
  2. Invoiceit's billing function works out the price from the business's region and opens a Paystack checkout. Card details are entered on Paystack, never in Invoiceit.
  3. After paying, Paystack sends the owner back to Invoiceit, which asks Paystack to confirm the payment and adds the paid time.
  4. If the browser never comes back (a closed tab or a dropped connection), Paystack's webhook confirms the payment anyway.
Who can pay
Owners only. Staff can't start a payment.
Price
₦20,000 a month in Nigeria. $15 a month everywhere else, charged in US dollars by card.
Safeguards
Each Paystack payment is counted once, even if it's confirmed twice. The database checks the currency and amount against the business's own price before adding any time.
Shared with Paystack
The owner's email address, the amount, and an internal business ID so the payment lands on the right business. No invoice or customer data.

WhatsApp Live

On any saved invoice, Share on WhatsApp opens WhatsApp with a short summary already typed in. You pick the chat and press send yourself; Invoiceit never sends anything on its own.

What the message says

Mainland Gadgets · Invoice 00042
Customer: Ada Okafor
Total: ₦ 13,500.00
Paid: ₦ 5,000.00
Left to pay: ₦ 8,500.00
Status: PART-PAID
Needs
WhatsApp on the phone, or WhatsApp Web or Desktop on a computer.
Sends
Text only. "Left to pay" appears only when there's a balance, and "Change" only when a customer overpaid. No PDF or link is attached.

Print and PDF Live

Print and Download PDF both open your device's print dialog. Print to a paper or receipt printer, or choose Save as PDF to get a file you can email or keep.

What prints
Only the invoice: business name, address and phone, invoice number, customer, items, payments, totals and notes. Buttons and menus are left out.
Where
On a saved invoice, and while writing a new one.
Works on
Any browser that can print. On phones, "Save as PDF" is usually under Share or Print, depending on the device.

Invoice links Live

Copy link on a saved invoice copies its web address, so a teammate can open the same invoice on another device.

Links are for your team, not your customers. Whoever opens one has to sign in to an account in the same business, or they won't see the invoice. To send a customer their invoice, use WhatsApp or a PDF.

Supabase Built on

Invoiceit stores its data and handles sign-in with Supabase. This isn't something you set up or connect: it's where every business's invoices, team and join codes live. Each business can only reach its own rows; the Overview explains how.

Not yet available

These are often asked about. None of them exist today:

  • Taking payments from your customers through Invoiceit.
  • Emailing or texting invoices from inside the app.
  • Public links that customers can open without an account.
  • Exporting invoices to CSV or syncing with accounting software.
  • Webhooks that notify your own systems of new invoices or payments. (The API itself is live: see the quickstart.)
