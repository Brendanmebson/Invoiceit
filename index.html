    Questions,
    answered

    Do I need a card to start the free trial?

    No. Sign up with your email, a password and your business name, and you get 14 days free. Each email gets one trial.

    How much does it cost after the trial?

    ₦20,000 a month for the whole business, with as many staff as you need. The owner can pay for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months at a time, and paying early just adds the time on top of what's left.

    How do I pay?

    Through Paystack, by card, bank transfer or USSD. The owner pays from the Business page inside the app.

    Is it only for shops that sell gadgets?

    No. Invoiceit works for any business that sells goods or services: boutiques, caterers, hardware stores, print shops, repairers and more. Each line is just an item, an optional serial or note, a quantity and a price.

    Can Invoiceit generate invoices automatically?

    Yes, from each sale. You enter the customer, the items and how they paid, and Invoiceit fills in your business details, the invoice number, the date, every total and the balance, ready to print or share. It doesn't yet create invoices on a schedule or from other systems.

    How do my staff join?

    The owner creates a one-time join code on the Business page and gives it to the staff member. Codes expire after 7 days. Staff can write invoices and record payments, but they can't see or change the business details.

    Can other businesses see my invoices?

    No. Each business sees only its own invoices, team and join codes. This is enforced in the database itself, not just hidden in the app.

    Can I print invoices or send them as a PDF?

    Yes. Download PDF opens your device's print dialog: print it, or choose “Save as PDF”. You can also send the summary to the customer on WhatsApp.

    What happens if I stop paying?

    When paid time runs out, the business is locked until the owner pays. If it stays unpaid for 14 more days, the business and all its invoices, team and codes are permanently deleted.
